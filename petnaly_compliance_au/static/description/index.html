Petnaly: Australian Compliance

State breeding rules and per-animal warnings for Australia.

What this module does

  • Rules for all 8 states/territories — data-driven and editable: max fertile females per premises, lifetime litter limits (and the lower limit when a C-section occurs), minimum sale age, microchip age, and breeder-ID requirement, each with a legal citation.
  • Per-animal warnings — a banner flags animals sold before the minimum age, missing a required microchip, or dams that exceed the lifetime litter limit — based on your farm's state.
  • Farm state setting on the company applies the right rules automatically.

Requires the free Petnaly: Breeding Core module. The bundled figures are a starting point — always verify against current local legislation.
