Petget: Customer Follow-up

Turn every sale into a relationship.

What this module does

  • Automatic follow-up tasks — when a puppy is marked sold, a series of care tasks (welcome call, check-ins, vaccination reminder, anniversary) is created with due dates and assigned to staff.
  • Human touchpoints, not spam — staff phone or message the customer and mark each task done; no automated emails.
  • Customer Follow-ups screen — see your to-dos, overdue and due-today filters, grouped by customer or staff.
  • Per-customer history — a Follow-ups button on each buyer, with a chatter log of every call.
  • Configurable steps — edit the follow-up templates and timing.

Requires the free Petget: Buyer & Sale module.
