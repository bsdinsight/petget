Petnaly: Health Screening

Hip/elbow, DNA and more — with breeding clearance warnings.

What this module does

  • Structured health tests per animal: hip & elbow scores (left/right/total with breed average), DNA/genetic, eye, heart, patella and more.
  • Not-for-Breeding flag and Main/Limited register status on each dog.
  • Smart breeding warnings — when you record a mating, the system flags a parent that is breeding-restricted or has no hip/elbow clearance on record.
  • Filter and compare — find dogs with clearances; compare scores against the breed average.

Requires the free Petnaly: Breeding Core module. Health data supports — never replaces — your veterinarian's advice.
